# About Me

B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student interested in Embedded Systems, IoT, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Electronics.

I have worked on Arduino-based projects, AI/ML projects, Verilog and RTL designs, and software prototypes as part of my academic learning, personal projects, and hackathon participation.

My projects include Smart Scan Radar System, SparseNPU Engine, HostelGuard AI, and NetCompass, covering areas such as Arduino, ultrasonic sensing, Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, Verilog, and network monitoring.

I am currently learning and improving my skills in C, C++, Python, Verilog, Embedded Systems, Arduino, ESP32, IoT, AI/ML, and digital design.

I participate in hackathons, technical events, workshops, and project-based activities to explore different areas of electronics and technology.

I am interested in Embedded Systems, IoT, AI-enabled hardware, microcontrollers, and hardware-software integration, with the goal of developing my skills toward a career in Embedded Systems.

I enjoy building, experimenting, and learning by working on practical technical projects.
